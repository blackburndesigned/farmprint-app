# Sample shop models: sources and license

Every model and picture in this folder was made for FarmPrint by `make_models.py` in this
folder. Each one is simple geometry written in code, and nothing was downloaded or traced
from someone else's design. They are released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain), so anyone can
print, change or reuse them, inside or outside FarmPrint.

| Flexi Dragon | Body, Wings, Head | Made for FarmPrint (`make_models.py`, `flexi_dragon`) | CC0 1.0 |
| Planter with Drip Tray | Drip Tray, Pot | Made for FarmPrint (`make_models.py`, `planter`) | CC0 1.0 |
| Cable Organizer | Grip Pad, Base | Made for FarmPrint (`make_models.py`, `cable_organizer`) | CC0 1.0 |
| Headphone Stand | Base, Post, Hanger | Made for FarmPrint (`make_models.py`, `headphone_stand`) | CC0 1.0 |
| Desk Tray | Tray, Dividers | Made for FarmPrint (`make_models.py`, `desk_tray`) | CC0 1.0 |
| Mountain Keychain | Tag, Inlay, Split Ring | Made for FarmPrint (`make_models.py`, `keychain`) | CC0 1.0 |

The customers in the sample orders are made up (a first name and an initial, with
`@example.com` addresses), as are the filament brand and printer names.

To rebuild everything: `pip install manifold3d numpy pillow`, then `python3 make_models.py`
from this folder.
