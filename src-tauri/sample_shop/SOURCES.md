# Sample shop models: sources and license

Every model and picture in this folder was made for FarmPrint by `make_models.py` in this
folder. Each one is a simple, generic shape written in code; nothing was downloaded from a
model site, traced, or based on a known designer's work. They are released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain): royalty-free,
and anyone may print, change, reuse and sell them commercially, inside or outside FarmPrint.

Any model added to the sample shop later must meet the same bar: made by us, or CC0 with
commercial use allowed. Nothing CC-BY-NC, and nothing from a known designer.

The customers in the sample orders are made up (a first name and an initial, with
`@example.com` addresses), as are the filament brand and printer names.

To rebuild everything: `pip install manifold3d numpy pillow`, then `python3 make_models.py`
from this folder.
